# Races — Practice Sets

> Companion to `09-Races.md`. Hints point to the section of the notes that contains the intended method — read the hint only after you've been stuck for a minute. Answer key with short solutions is at the bottom.
>
> **TITA** = type-in-the-answer (no options). **MCQ** = choose one.
>
> Suggested timing: 1.5 min/question for Sets A–E, and attempt the Mixed Set in one timed block.
>
> **Every race question is a speed ratio in disguise.** "A beats B by x metres in a d-metre race" means that in the same time A covers d while B covers d − x, so the speed ratio is d : (d − x) (§1).
>
> Read the language carefully: a beat given as a distance and a beat given as a time are different data, and "gives a start" reverses who covers what (§2).

---

## Set A — Beats given as a distance

**A1.** (TITA) In a 100 m race, A beats B by 20 m. When A finishes, how far has B run (in metres)?

*Hint: §2 — "beats by 20 m" describes where B is at the moment A finishes.*

**A2.** (MCQ) In a 500 m race, A beats B by 50 m. The ratio of their speeds is

- (a) 10 : 9
- (b) 9 : 10
- (c) 10 : 1
- (d) 50 : 1

*Hint: §3 — same time, so the speeds are in the ratio of the distances covered.*

**A3.** (TITA) In a 200 m race, A beats B by 25 m. If A runs at 8 m/s, find B's speed (in m/s).

*Hint: §3 — find A's time, then divide B's distance by that same time.*

**A4.** (MCQ) In a 1 km race, A beats B by 100 m. At the same speeds, in a 2 km race A beats B by

- (a) 100 m
- (b) 150 m
- (c) 200 m
- (d) 250 m

*Hint: §3 — the speed ratio is fixed, so the beat distance scales with the race length.*

**A5.** (TITA) In a 400 m race, A beats B by 40 m, and in a separate 400 m race B beats C by 50 m. By how many metres does A beat C in a 400 m race?

*Hint: §5 — chain the two speed ratios. Do not add the two beats.*

## Set B — Beats given as a time

**B1.** (TITA) In a 500 m race, A beats B by 10 seconds. If B takes 60 seconds, find A's time (in seconds).

*Hint: §4 — a beat in time is simply the difference of the two finishing times.*

**B2.** (MCQ) In a 1000 m race, A beats B by 100 m or 10 seconds. B's speed is

- (a) 8 m/s
- (b) 10 m/s
- (c) 12 m/s
- (d) 12.5 m/s

*Hint: §4 — the two statements describe the same gap, so B covers those 100 m in those 10 seconds.*

**B3.** (TITA) In a 1000 m race, A beats B by 100 m or 10 seconds. Find A's time for the race (in seconds).

*Hint: §4 — get B's speed from the twin statement, then B's total time, then subtract the beat.*

**B4.** (MCQ) In a 300 m race, A beats B by 30 m or 6 seconds. A's speed is

- (a) 5 m/s
- (b) 5.56 m/s
- (c) 6 m/s
- (d) 6.5 m/s

*Hint: §4 — B's speed comes from the twin statement; A's time is B's time minus 6 seconds.*

**B5.** (TITA) A covers a 300 m course in 45 seconds and B in 60 seconds. By how many metres does A beat B?

*Hint: §4 — in A's 45 seconds, work out how far B has got.*

---

## Set C — Chaining three runners

**C1.** (TITA) In a 100 m race, A beats B by 10 m and B beats C by 10 m. By how many metres does A beat C?

*Hint: §5 — multiply the ratios, don't add the beats. The answer is deliberately not 20.*

**C2.** (MCQ) In a 200 m race, A beats B by 20 m and A beats C by 38 m. In a 180 m race, B beats C by

- (a) 9 m
- (b) 18 m
- (c) 20 m
- (d) 24 m

*Hint: §5 — express both B and C relative to A, then form the B : C ratio directly.*

**C3.** (TITA) In a 500 m race, A beats B by 50 m, and B beats C by 50 m in a 500 m race. By how many metres does A beat C over 500 m?

*Hint: §5 — again the product of the ratios.*

**C4.** (MCQ) A runs 1 km in 4 minutes 54 seconds and B in 5 minutes. In a 1 km race A beats B by

- (a) 15 m
- (b) 20 m
- (c) 25 m
- (d) 30 m

*Hint: §4 — convert both to seconds, then find B's position at A's finishing time.*

## Set D — Dead heats and head starts

**D1.** (TITA) A runs 5/4 as fast as B. If A gives B a head start of 60 m, how long (in metres) must the race be so that both finish together?

*Hint: §6 — a dead heat means equal times: A covers d while B covers d − 60.*

**D2.** (MCQ) A can run 1 km in 3 minutes 10 seconds and B in 3 minutes 20 seconds. For a dead heat, the head start A must give B is

- (a) 30 m
- (b) 40 m
- (c) 50 m
- (d) 60 m

*Hint: §6 — the head start equals the beat A would otherwise inflict.*

**D3.** (TITA) In a 200 m race, A gives B a start of 10 seconds and still beats him by 20 m. If B's speed is 5 m/s, find A's time for the race (in seconds).

*Hint: §7 — B runs for A's time plus the 10-second start, and covers 180 m in that period.*

**D4.** (MCQ) A gives B a start of 20 m in a 100 m race and still wins by 5 m. The ratio of their speeds is

- (a) 4 : 3
- (b) 5 : 4
- (c) 5 : 3
- (d) 3 : 2

*Hint: §7 — B only has 80 m to cover, and finishes 5 m short of the line when A arrives.*

**D5.** (TITA) In a 1000 m race A beats B by 50 m. In a race of the same distance where A instead gives B a head start of 50 m, by how many metres does A win? (Enter 0 for a dead heat.)

*Hint: §6 — the head start exactly cancels the advantage that produced the original beat.*

## Set E — Games of points and laps

**E1.** (TITA) In a game of 100 points, A can give B 20 points. In the time A scores 100, how many points does B score?

*Hint: §8 — "gives 20 points" means B is on 80 when A reaches 100.*

**E2.** (MCQ) In a game of 80 points, A can give B 10 points and C 20 points. In a game of 100 points, A can give C

- (a) 20 points
- (b) 25 points
- (c) 30 points
- (d) 33.33 points

*Hint: §8 — build the A : C ratio from the 80-point game, then rescale to 100.*

**E3.** (TITA) In a game of 100 points, A can give B 20 points and B can give C 25 points. How many points can A give C in a game of 100?

*Hint: §8 + §5 — chain the ratios exactly as with runners.*

**E4.** (MCQ) On a 400 m circular track, A and B run at 8 m/s and 6 m/s in the same direction from the same point. A first laps B after

- (a) 100 s
- (b) 150 s
- (c) 200 s
- (d) 400 s

*Hint: §9 — lapping means gaining a full circumference at the relative speed.*

**E5.** (TITA) In a race on a 500 m circular track, A completes 3 laps in the time B completes 2.5 laps. By how many metres does A beat B?

*Hint: §9 — the gap is half a lap.*

## Mixed Set — exam feel (13 minutes)

**M1.** (TITA) In a 300 m race, A beats B by 30 m. Find the ratio of A's speed to B's speed, as a fraction.

**M2.** (MCQ) In a 400 m race, A beats B by 25 m and B beats C by 20 m. A beats C by

- (a) 40 m
- (b) 42.5 m
- (c) 43.75 m
- (d) 45 m

**M3.** (TITA) A runs 4/3 as fast as B. If A gives B a start of 30 m, how long (in metres) must the course be for a dead heat?

**M4.** (MCQ) In a 100 m race, A beats B by 4 seconds. If B takes 25 seconds, A's speed is

- (a) 4 m/s
- (b) 4.76 m/s
- (c) 5 m/s
- (d) 5.25 m/s

**M5.** (TITA) In a game of 100 points, A can give B 25 points. In a game of 60 points, how many points can A give B?

**M6.** (MCQ) The speeds of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 4. A is given a head start of 140 m and the race ends in a dead heat. The length of the course is

- (a) 420 m
- (b) 500 m
- (c) 560 m
- (d) 600 m

**M7.** (TITA) In a 1 km race, A beats B by 40 m and A beats C by 76 m. By how many metres does B beat C in a 1 km race?

**M8.** (MCQ) In a 200 m race, A gives B a start of 5 seconds and beats him by 10 m. If A's speed is 8 m/s, B's speed is

- (a) 6 m/s
- (b) 6.33 m/s
- (c) 6.67 m/s
- (d) 7 m/s

# Answer key

## Set A

**A1 — 80.** B has covered **80 m**.

**A2 — (a) 10 : 9.** 500 : 450 = **10 : 9**.

**A3 — 7.** A takes 200/8 = 25 s, in which B covers 175 m ⟹ B's speed = **7 m/s**.

**A4 — (c) 200 m.** Speeds are 10 : 9 ⟹ when A covers 2000 m, B covers 1800 m ⟹ a beat of **200 m**.

**A5 — 85.** A : B = 10 : 9 and B : C = 8 : 7 ⟹ A : C = 80 : 63 ⟹ when A runs 400, C runs 315 ⟹ a beat of **85 m**. (Adding 40 + 50 = 90 is the planted error.)

## Set B

**B1 — 50.** 60 − 10 = **50 seconds**.

**B2 — (b) 10 m/s.** 100/10 = **10 m/s**.

**B3 — 90.** B's speed = 10 m/s ⟹ B takes 100 s ⟹ A takes **90 seconds**.

**B4 — (b) 5.56 m/s.** B: 30/6 = 5 m/s ⟹ B takes 60 s ⟹ A takes 54 s ⟹ A's speed = 300/54 = **5.56 m/s**.

**B5 — 75.** B covers 300 × 45/60 = 225 m ⟹ a beat of **75 m**.

## Set C

**C1 — 19.** A : B = 10 : 9 and B : C = 10 : 9 ⟹ when A runs 100, B is at 90 and C at 81 ⟹ a beat of **19 m**.

**C2 — (b) 18 m.** B = 0.90A and C = 0.81A ⟹ B : C = 10 : 9 ⟹ in a 180 m race C reaches 162 ⟹ a beat of **18 m**.

**C3 — 95.** A : B = B : C = 10 : 9 ⟹ when A runs 500, B is at 450 and C at 405 ⟹ a beat of **95 m**.

**C4 — (b) 20 m.** 294 s vs 300 s ⟹ B covers 1000 × 294/300 = 980 m ⟹ a beat of **20 m**.

## Set D

**D1 — 300.** 0.8d = d − 60 ⟹ 0.2d = 60 ⟹ d = **300 m**.

**D2 — (c) 50 m.** 190 s vs 200 s ⟹ in 190 s B covers 950 m ⟹ head start = **50 m**.

**D3 — 26.** B covers 180 m at 5 m/s = 36 s of running ⟹ A's time = 36 − 10 = **26 seconds**.

**D4 — (a) 4 : 3.** A covers 100 m while B covers 75 m ⟹ **4 : 3**.

**D5 — 0 — it is a dead heat.** Speeds 20 : 19. B must cover 950 m; in A's 1000 m, B covers 19/20 × 1000 = 950 m ⟹ they finish together, so A wins by **0 m**.

## Set E

**E1 — 80.** **80 points**.

**E2 — (b) 25 points.** A : C = 80 : 60 = 4 : 3 ⟹ in a 100-point game C scores 75 ⟹ A gives **25 points**.

**E3 — 40.** A : B = 5 : 4 and B : C = 4 : 3 ⟹ A : C = 5 : 3 ⟹ C scores 60 ⟹ A gives **40 points**.

**E4 — (c) 200 s.** 400/(8 − 6) = **200 seconds**.

**E5 — 250.** A covers 1500 m and B 1250 m ⟹ a beat of **250 m**.

## Mixed Set

**M1 — 10/9.** 300 : 270 = **10/9**.

**M2 — (c) 43.75 m.** A : B = 16 : 15 and B : C = 20 : 19 ⟹ when A runs 400, B is at 375 and C at 356.25 ⟹ a beat of **43.75 m**.

**M3 — 120.** 0.75d = d − 30 ⟹ d = **120 m**.

**M4 — (b) 4.76 m/s.** A takes 21 s ⟹ 100/21 = **4.76 m/s**.

**M5 — 15.** A : B = 4 : 3 ⟹ in 60 points B scores 45 ⟹ A gives **15 points**.

**M6 — (c) 560 m.** (d − 140)/d = 3/4 ⟹ 4d − 560 = 3d ⟹ d = **560 m**.

**M7 — 37.5.** B : C = 960 : 924 ⟹ when B runs 1000, C reaches 962.5 ⟹ a beat of **37.5 m**. (76 − 40 = 36 is the planted error.)

**M8 — (b) 6.33 m/s.** A takes 25 s ⟹ B runs for 30 s covering 190 m ⟹ **6.33 m/s**.

**Common error audit** — if you got a question wrong, find it here before moving on:

| Question | The error it is designed to catch |
|---|---|
| A2, A3, A4, M1 | not converting the beat into a speed ratio before doing anything else (§1, §3) |
| A5, C1, C2, C3, M2, M7 | adding two beats instead of multiplying the two speed ratios (§5) |
| B2, B3, B4 | treating the distance-beat and time-beat as separate facts rather than as one shared gap (§4) |
| D1, D2, M3, M6 | getting the direction of a head start backwards — the runner given the start covers less (§6, §7) |
| D3, M8 | forgetting that a time head start extends the slower runner's running time |
| D5 | assuming a head start equal to the beat still leaves a winner; it produces a dead heat exactly |
| E2, E3, M5 | rescaling a points game by subtracting instead of by reapplying the ratio (§8) |
| E4, E5 | confusing lapping with meeting, or losing a half-lap in the count (§9) |
