# Circles — Practice Sets

> Companion to `18-Circles.md`. Hints point to the section of the notes that contains the intended method — read the hint only after you've been stuck for a minute. Answer key with short solutions is at the bottom.
>
> **TITA** = type-in-the-answer (no options). **MCQ** = choose one.
>
> Suggested timing: 2.0 min/question for Sets A–A, and attempt the Mixed Set in one timed block.
>
> **Join the centre to everything.** Radii are free equal lengths, and most circle figures open up once three or four are drawn (§2).
>
> A diameter means a right angle; a tangent with a chord means the alternate segment angle. Mark both on sight (§2, §3).

---

## Set A — Chords, tangents and cyclic quadrilaterals

**A1.** (TITA) A chord of length 16 cm lies 6 cm from the centre of a circle. Find the radius (in cm).

*Hint: §2 — the perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord, giving a right triangle with legs 8 and 6.*

**A2.** (MCQ) Two circles of radii 5 and 3 touch externally. The distance between their centres is

- (a) 2
- (b) 4
- (c) 8
- (d) 15

*Hint: §6 — external touching means the centre distance is the sum; internal would be the difference.*

**A3.** (TITA) From an external point 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm, find the length of a tangent (in cm).

*Hint: §3, tangents — the tangent is perpendicular to the radius, so use Pythagoras. Look for the 5-12-13 triple.*

**A4.** (MCQ) In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, angle A = 80°. Then angle C is

- (a) 80°
- (b) 90°
- (c) 100°
- (d) 110°

*Hint: §4, cyclic quadrilaterals — opposite angles are supplementary.*

**A5.** (MCQ) The area of a quarter-circle sector of radius 14 cm (π = 22/7) is

- (a) 77 cm²
- (b) 154 cm²
- (c) 196 cm²
- (d) 308 cm²

*Hint: §1 — a quarter of πr².*

---

## Mixed Set — exam feel (4 minutes)

**M1.** (MCQ) The circumference of a circle is 44 cm (π = 22/7). Its area is

- (a) 77 cm²
- (b) 154 cm²
- (c) 308 cm²
- (d) 616 cm²

**M2.** (MCQ) A circle is inscribed in a square of side 14. The area between them (π = 22/7) is

- (a) 42
- (b) 49
- (c) 56
- (d) 63

---

# Answer key

## Set A

**A1 — 10.** √(8² + 6²) = **10 cm**.

**A2 — (c) 8.** 5 + 3 = **8**. (2 is the internal-touching answer, planted.)

**A3 — 12.** √(13² − 5²) = **12 cm**.

**A4 — (c) 100°.** 180 − 80 = **100°**.

**A5 — (b) 154 cm².** ¼ × (22/7) × 196 = **154 cm²**.

## Mixed Set

**M1 — (b) 154 cm².** 2πr = 44 ⟹ r = 7 ⟹ area = (22/7)(49) = **154 cm²**.

**M2 — (a) 42.** 196 − (22/7)(49) = 196 − 154 = **42**.

---

**Common error audit** — if you got a question wrong, find it here before moving on:

| Question | The error it is designed to catch |
|---|---|
| A1, A3 | grinding through algebra where a Pythagorean triple was staring at you (§2, §3) |
| A2 | using the difference of the radii for externally touching circles — externally means the sum (§6) |
| A4 | forgetting that opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary (§4) |
| A5, M1 | confusing sector with segment, or circumference with area (§1) |
| M2 | taking the square's diagonal as the inscribed circle's diameter — it is the side (§5, §7) |
