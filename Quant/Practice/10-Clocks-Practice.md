# Clocks — Practice Sets

> Companion to `10-Clocks.md`. Hints point to the section of the notes that contains the intended method — read the hint only after you've been stuck for a minute. Answer key with short solutions is at the bottom.
>
> **TITA** = type-in-the-answer (no options). **MCQ** = choose one.
>
> Suggested timing: 1.5 min/question for Sets A–D, and attempt the Mixed Set in one timed block.
>
> **One formula does almost everything: angle = |30H − 5.5M|.** The hour hand moves 0.5°/min and the minute hand 6°/min, and 30H − 5.5M is just the difference (§2).
>
> If the formula returns more than 180°, subtract from 360° to get the smaller angle — unless the question explicitly asks for the reflex angle.

---

## Set A — The angle between the hands

**A1.** (TITA) Find the angle (in degrees) between the hands of a clock at 3:20.

*Hint: §2 — |30(3) − 5.5(20)|. The common wrong answer of 0° assumes the hour hand stays on the 3.*

**A2.** (MCQ) The angle between the hands at 4:40 is

- (a) 90°
- (b) 100°
- (c) 110°
- (d) 120°

*Hint: §2 — |120 − 220|.*

**A3.** (TITA) Find the angle (in degrees) between the hands at 7:10.

*Hint: §2 — |210 − 55|. Still under 180, so no correction is needed.*

**A4.** (MCQ) The angle between the hands at 9:30 is

- (a) 95°
- (b) 100°
- (c) 105°
- (d) 110°

*Hint: §2 — the hour hand is halfway between 9 and 10, not on the 9.*

**A5.** (TITA) Find the reflex angle (in degrees) between the hands at 2:50.

*Hint: §2 — the formula gives 215°, which is already the reflex angle; the smaller one is 145°. Read which is wanted.*

## Set B — Coincidence, opposition and right angles

**B1.** (TITA) At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock do the hands coincide? Give the number of minutes past 3, to two decimals.

*Hint: §3 — coincidence means the angle is 0, so 30H = 5.5M.*

**B2.** (MCQ) Between 2 and 3 o'clock, the hands point in exactly opposite directions at

- (a) 43.64 minutes past 2
- (b) 40 minutes past 2
- (c) 45 minutes past 2
- (d) 38.18 minutes past 2

*Hint: §3 — opposition means an angle of 180°, so solve |60 − 5.5M| = 180.*

**B3.** (TITA) At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock are the hands first at right angles? Give minutes past 4, to two decimals.

*Hint: §4 — solve |120 − 5.5M| = 90. There are two solutions in the hour; the question wants the earlier.*

**B4.** (MCQ) How many times in 12 hours do the hands of a clock coincide?

- (a) 11
- (b) 12
- (c) 22
- (d) 24

*Hint: §3 — between 11 and 1 there is only one coincidence, at 12 o'clock. That is what costs the twelfth.*

**B5.** (TITA) How many times in 24 hours are the hands of a clock at right angles?

*Hint: §3 — 22 in 12 hours, by the same logic that gives 11 coincidences.*

## Set C — Faulty clocks

**C1.** (TITA) A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If it is set right at 8 a.m., how many minutes will it be ahead at 8 p.m. the same day?

*Hint: §5 — a uniform gain accumulates linearly over the elapsed hours.*

**C2.** (MCQ) A clock gains 10 seconds per hour and is set right at 6 a.m. At the true time of 6 p.m. the same day, it shows

- (a) 6:01 p.m.
- (b) 6:02 p.m.
- (c) 6:05 p.m.
- (d) 6:10 p.m.

*Hint: §5 — convert the total gain into minutes at the end.*

**C3.** (TITA) A clock is set right at 5 a.m. and loses 16 minutes in 24 hours. When the clock shows 10 p.m. on the fourth day, how many minutes past 10 p.m. is the true time?

*Hint: §5 — the clock shows 1424 minutes for every 1440 true minutes, so scale the shown interval up by 1440/1424.*

**C4.** (MCQ) Two clocks are set right at noon. One gains 2 minutes a day and the other loses 3 minutes a day. They will be 1 hour apart after

- (a) 10 days
- (b) 12 days
- (c) 15 days
- (d) 20 days

*Hint: §5 — the gap grows at the sum of the two rates, since they drift in opposite directions.*

**C5.** (TITA) A watch gains uniformly. It was 3 minutes slow at noon on Monday and 3 minutes fast at 9 p.m. the following Monday. How many hours after noon on Monday did it show the correct time?

*Hint: §5 — the total drift is 6 minutes over the whole interval; the correct moment is where half of it has accumulated.*

## Set D — Mirror images and counting within a window

**D1.** (MCQ) A clock seen in a mirror appears to show 8:20. The actual time is

- (a) 3:40
- (b) 4:40
- (c) 3:20
- (d) 4:20

*Hint: §6 — subtract the shown time from 11:60 (i.e. 12:00), which is the reflection rule for any time other than exactly 12.*

**D2.** (TITA) The mirror image of a clock shows 2:30. Express the actual time as the number of minutes past 9.

*Hint: §6 — the same subtraction; here it lands on a whole half-hour.*

**D3.** (MCQ) A clock in a mirror appears to show 10:10. The actual time is

- (a) 1:50
- (b) 2:50
- (c) 1:10
- (d) 2:10

*Hint: §6 — 11:60 minus the shown time.*

**D4.** (TITA) How many times between 3 p.m. and 9 p.m. do the hands of a clock coincide?

*Hint: §7 — coincidences recur every 720/11 minutes; count how many land inside the window.*

**D5.** (MCQ) After the hands of a clock coincide, they next coincide after

- (a) 60 minutes
- (b) 65 minutes
- (c) 65.45 minutes
- (d) 66 minutes

*Hint: §3 — 11 coincidences spread over 12 hours.*

**M1.** (TITA) Find the angle (in degrees) between the hands at 5:30.

**M2.** (MCQ) The angle between the hands at 8:24 is

- (a) 96°
- (b) 102°
- (c) 108°
- (d) 114°

**M3.** (TITA) At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock do the hands coincide? Give minutes past 6, to two decimals.

**M4.** (MCQ) How many times in 24 hours do the hands of a clock point in opposite directions?

- (a) 11
- (b) 22
- (c) 24
- (d) 44

**M5.** (TITA) A clock gains 4 minutes every hour and is set right at 9 a.m. How many minutes past 9 p.m. does it show at the true time of 9 p.m. the same day?

**M6.** (MCQ) A clock seen in a mirror shows 4:35. The actual time is

- (a) 7:25
- (b) 8:25

- (d) 8:35

**M7.** (TITA) At what time between 10 and 11 o'clock are the hands at right angles for the second time? Give minutes past 10, to two decimals.

**M8.** (MCQ) A clock loses 5 minutes every hour and is set right at 6 a.m. At the true time of 6 p.m. the same day, it shows

- (a) 5:00 p.m.
- (b) 5:30 p.m.
- (c) 5:45 p.m.
- (d) 6:00 p.m.

# Answer key

## Set A

**A1 — 20°.** |90 − 110| = **20°**.

**A2 — (b) 100°.** |120 − 220| = **100°**.

**A3 — 155°.** |210 − 55| = **155°**.

**A4 — (c) 105°.** |270 − 165| = **105°**.

**A5 — 215°.** |60 − 275| = 215° ⟹ the reflex angle is **215°** (the smaller angle being 145°).

## Set B

**B1 — 16.36 minutes past 3.** 5.5M = 90 ⟹ M = 90/5.5 = **16.36 minutes past 3** (i.e. 3:16 and 21.8 s).

**B2 — (a) 43.64 minutes past 2.** 5.5M = 240 ⟹ M = **43.64 minutes past 2**.

**B3 — 5.45 minutes past 4.** 5.5M = 30 ⟹ M = **5.45** (the other right angle falls at 38.18 minutes past 4).

**B4 — (a) 11.** **11 times** — once in each hour except that the 11 o'clock and 12 o'clock cases are the same event.

**B5 — 44 times.** 22 per 12 hours ⟹ **44 times** in a day.

## Set C

**C1 — 60 minutes.** 12 hours × 5 min = **60 minutes** ahead.

**C2 — (b) 6:02 p.m..** 12 × 10 = 120 s = 2 minutes ⟹ it shows **6:02 p.m.**

**C3 — 60 minutes (the true time is 11 p.m.).** Shown interval = 89 hours = 5340 min ⟹ true = 5340 × 1440/1424 = 5400 min = 90 hours ⟹ true time is 11 p.m., i.e. **60 minutes** past 10 p.m.

**C4 — (b) 12 days.** Relative drift = 5 min/day ⟹ 60/5 = **12 days**.

**C5 — 88.5 hours.** Interval = 7 days 9 hours = 177 hours for a 6-minute swing ⟹ correct at 177 × 3/6 = **88.5 hours** after noon on Monday.

## Set D

**D1 — (a) 3:40.** 11:60 − 8:20 = **3:40**.

**D2 — 30 minutes past 9 (9:30).** 12:00 − 2:30 = 9:30 ⟹ **30 minutes past 9**.

**D3 — (a) 1:50.** 11:60 − 10:10 = **1:50**.

**D4 — 6 times.** Roughly at 3:16, 4:22, 5:27, 6:33, 7:38 and 8:44 ⟹ **6 times**.

**D5 — (c) 65.45 minutes.** 720/11 = **65.45 minutes**.

## Mixed Set

**M1 — 15°.** |150 − 165| = **15°**.

**M2 — (c) 108°.** |240 − 132| = **108°**.

**M3 — 32.73 minutes past 6.** M = 180/5.5 = **32.73 minutes past 6**.

**M4 — (b) 22.** **22 times**.

**M5 — 48 minutes.** 12 × 4 = **48 minutes** (it shows 9:48 p.m.).

**M6 — (a) 7:25.** 11:60 − 4:35 = **7:25**.

**M7 — 38.18 minutes past 10.** 5.5M = 210 ⟹ M = **38.18** (the first right angle was at 5.45 minutes past 10).

**M8 — (a) 5:00 p.m..** 12 × 5 = 60 minutes lost ⟹ it shows **5:00 p.m.**

**Common error audit** — if you got a question wrong, find it here before moving on:

| Question | The error it is designed to catch |
|---|---|
| A1, A2, A4, M1, M2 | treating the hour hand as fixed on the hour mark instead of advancing 0.5° per minute (§2) |
| A5 | reporting the smaller angle where the reflex angle was asked, or vice versa |
| B1, B2, M3 | using 6M instead of 5.5M — the relative speed of the hands is 5.5°/min, not 6 (§3) |
| B3, M7 | finding only one of the two right angles in an hour, or picking the wrong one (§4) |
| B4, B5, M4 | answering 12 or 24 by assuming one event per hour (§3) |
| C1, C2, C5, M5, M8 | mixing the units of the drift, or applying it to the wrong number of hours (§5) |
| C3 | scaling the shown interval down instead of up for a slow clock |
| C4 | subtracting the two drift rates when the clocks drift in opposite directions |
| D1, D2, D3, M6 | subtracting from 12:00 without borrowing correctly — use 11:60 (§6) |
| D4, D5 | assuming coincidences fall exactly one hour apart rather than 65.45 minutes (§7) |
