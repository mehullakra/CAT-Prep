# Mensuration — Practice Sets

> Companion to `20-Mensuration.md`. Hints point to the section of the notes that contains the intended method — read the hint only after you've been stuck for a minute. Answer key with short solutions is at the bottom.
>
> **TITA** = type-in-the-answer (no options). **MCQ** = choose one.
>
> Suggested timing: 2.0 min/question for Sets A–A, and attempt the Mixed Set in one timed block.
>
> **Write the conservation equation before any numbers.** Volume before = volume after is the whole method for a third of this topic (§3).
>
> Cancel π and common factors symbolically first, and check the units line — mm versus cm is where these questions are lost (§1).

## Set A — Solids

**A1.** (TITA) Find the volume of a cone of radius 7 and height 12 (π = 22/7).

*Hint: §1 — ⅓πr²h. Dropping the ⅓ is the standard slip.*

**A2.** (MCQ) The total surface area of a cube of edge 5 cm is

- (a) 100 cm²
- (b) 125 cm²
- (c) 150 cm²
- (d) 180 cm²

*Hint: §1 — six faces. 125 is the volume, planted.*

**A3.** (TITA) A sphere of radius 6 has volume kπ. Find k.

*Hint: §1 — V = (4/3)πr³, so k = (4/3)r³.*

**A4.** (MCQ) A cylinder of radius 7 and height 10 has curved surface area (π = 22/7)

- (a) 220
- (b) 440
- (c) 480
- (d) 880

*Hint: §1 — curved surface is 2πrh; the total surface would add the two circular ends.*

**A5.** (TITA) A solid metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into spheres of radius 2 cm. How many small spheres are formed?

*Hint: §3 — volume is conserved, and volume scales as the cube of the radius.*

**A6.** (MCQ) A cuboid measures 8 × 6 × 5. The length of its space diagonal is

- (a) 5√5
- (b) 10
- (c) 11
- (d) 12

*Hint: §1 — √(l² + b² + h²), the three-dimensional Pythagoras.*

## Mixed Set — exam feel (5 minutes)

**M1.** (MCQ) A cone and a cylinder have the same radius and the same height. The ratio of their volumes is

- (a) 1 : 2
- (b) 1 : 3
- (c) 2 : 3
- (d) 3 : 1

**M2.** (TITA) The radii of two spheres are in the ratio 2 : 3. Find the ratio of their surface areas, expressed as larger ÷ smaller.

**M3.** (TITA) A cylindrical tank of radius 7 m and height 5 m is full of water. Find the volume in cubic metres (π = 22/7).

---

# Answer key

## Set A

**A1 — 616.** ⅓ × (22/7) × 49 × 12 = **616**.

**A2 — (c) 150 cm².** 6 × 25 = **150 cm²**.

**A3 — 288.** (4/3) × 216 = **288**.

**A4 — (b) 440.** 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = **440**.

**A5 — 27.** (6/2)³ = **27**.

**A6 — (a) 5√5.** √(64 + 36 + 25) = √125 = **5√5** ≈ 11.18.

## Mixed Set

**M1 — (b) 1 : 3.** ⅓πr²h : πr²h = **1 : 3**.

**M2 — 2.25 (i.e. 9 : 4).** (3/2)² = **2.25**, i.e. 9 : 4. (Cubing gives 3.375 — the volume ratio, planted.)

**M3 — 770.** (22/7)(49)(5) = **770 m³**.

**Common error audit** — if you got a question wrong, find it here before moving on:

| Question | The error it is designed to catch |
|---|---|
| A1, A2, A4 | confusing volume with surface area, or dropping the ⅓ in the cone (§1) |
| A5 | scaling volumes linearly instead of by the cube of the radius (§2, §3) |
| A6 | using a face diagonal where the space diagonal √(l²+b²+h²) was asked (§1) |
| M1 | forgetting the ⅓ when comparing a cone with a cylinder of the same radius and height (§1) |
| M2 | scaling a surface area by k instead of k² (§2) |
