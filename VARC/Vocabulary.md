## Praise and reverence

**laud** *(verb)* — to praise highly, especially in public.

Syn: extol, commend, acclaim · Ant: censure, denigrate

*“Critics lauded the film for its restraint.”*

**extol** *(verb)* — to praise enthusiastically and at length.

Syn: glorify, exalt, eulogise · Ant: disparage, belittle

*“He extolled the virtues of a slower life.”*

**venerate** *(verb)* — to regard with deep respect, often approaching worship.

Syn: revere, esteem, honour · Ant: scorn, despise

*“The text is venerated by scholars of every school.”*

**encomium** *(noun)* — a formal expression of high praise.

Syn: eulogy, panegyric, tribute · Ant: diatribe, censure

*“Her retirement drew encomiums from across the industry.”*

**acclaim** *(noun)* — enthusiastic and public approval.

Syn: plaudits, applause, commendation · Ant: derision, opprobrium

*“The novel met with immediate critical acclaim.”*
